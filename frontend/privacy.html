Privacy Policy

At Padhaan, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, education details, and other relevant information required to match you with suitable scholarships.

The information you provide is used to identify relevant scholarship opportunities and assist you in the application process. We may also contact you regarding updates, opportunities, or important information related to your applications.

We take appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. Your data is handled securely and only accessible to authorized personnel.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except when necessary to provide our services or comply with legal obligations.

User Consent

By using our platform and submitting your information, you consent to the collection and use of your data as described in this policy.

Note

"Personal Information" refers to any information that can identify an individual directly or indirectly.
